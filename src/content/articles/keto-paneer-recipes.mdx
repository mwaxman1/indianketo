## Why Paneer Is the Perfect Keto Food

No food defines Indian keto like paneer. This fresh Indian cheese is a nutritional unicorn: high in protein, rich in fat, and almost zero carbs. A 100g serving delivers:

- **18–20g protein** — more than two eggs
- **20–22g fat** — mostly healthy saturated and monounsaturated fats
- **Under 2g net carbs** — essentially zero
- **Calcium, phosphorus, and selenium** — bone health and immune support

Paneer grills, fries, simmers, scrambles, and stuffs. It absorbs spices beautifully and works in everything from rich Mughlai curries to quick street-food snacks. For vegetarians on keto, paneer is the foundation.

## Buying and Preparing Paneer

**Store-bought vs. homemade:** Both work. Store-bought paneer (Amul, Mother Dairy, Gowardhan) is consistent and convenient. Homemade is fresher — use full-fat milk and add a splash of cream before curdling for extra richness.

**To make at home:** Boil 2 liters full-fat milk. Add 2–3 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar, stir until curdled. Strain through muslin cloth, rinse with cold water, press under weight for 1–2 hours.

**Storage:** Refrigerate submerged in water for 3–4 days (change water daily). Freeze cubed for up to 3 months.

## 10+ Keto Paneer Recipes

### 1. Paneer Butter Masala — ~5g net carbs

The crown jewel. Paneer cubes in a velvety tomato-cream gravy finished with butter and kasuri methi. Sauté onion in butter, add ginger-garlic paste and tomato puree with turmeric, chili powder, and garam masala. Cook until oil separates, add water, simmer, then add paneer and cream. Finish with crushed kasuri methi and a pat of butter. Skip the sugar — cream and butter provide enough richness.

### 2. Palak Paneer — ~4g net carbs

The green powerhouse — naturally keto with zero modifications. Blanch spinach, plunge into ice water (keeps it bright green), then blend with green chilies. Sauté cumin and onion in ghee, add ginger-garlic paste and spices, then the spinach puree. Simmer 5 minutes, add paneer cubes, finish with cream. Don't overcook the spinach — it should stay vibrant.

### 3. Paneer Tikka — ~3g net carbs

Smoky, charred paneer cubes with bell peppers. Marinate paneer and peppers in hung curd (strained yogurt — fewer carbs) with mustard oil, chili powder, cumin, chaat masala, and garam masala. Thread onto skewers, grill at 220°C for 12–15 minutes. Finish with lemon and chaat masala.

### 4. Paneer Bhurji — ~3g net carbs

Indian-style scrambled paneer — ready in 15 minutes. Sauté cumin, onion, and green chilies in ghee. Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato, turmeric, and garam masala. Cook until tomato softens, add crumbled paneer, stir 3–4 minutes. Garnish with coriander. Add scrambled eggs for extra protein, or top with cheese for more fat.

### 5. Methi Paneer — ~3g net carbs

Fresh fenugreek leaves and paneer in a light cream sauce. Salt the methi leaves, squeeze out water (reduces bitterness). Sauté cumin and onion in ghee, add ginger-garlic paste and spices, then methi. Cook 5 minutes, add water and simmer, then add paneer and cream. If using dried kasuri methi instead, add it at the end — it burns easily.

### 6. Kadai Paneer — ~5g net carbs

Rustic, semi-dry masala with bell peppers. Dry-roast and crush coriander seeds for authentic flavor. Sauté onion and bell pepper briefly (keep them crunchy), set aside. Cook tomato puree with spices until oil separates, then add paneer, vegetables, and cream. Toss and serve. Great with keto roti or on its own.

### 7. Shahi Paneer — ~5g net carbs

"Royal" paneer in a nut-based cream gravy. Blend soaked almonds with boiled onion into a paste. Sauté whole spices (cardamom, cinnamon, clove) in ghee, add ginger-garlic paste, then the almond-onion paste with turmeric and white pepper. Cook 5–7 minutes, add water, simmer, then add paneer and cream. Almonds replace the higher-carb cashews of traditional recipes.

### 8. Paneer Bhurji Stuffed Peppers — ~4g net carbs

Bell peppers stuffed with spicy paneer bhurji and baked. Make paneer bhurji (recipe #4), stuff into halved green bell peppers, top with grated cheese, and bake at 200°C for 15–20 minutes. These reheat beautifully — great for meal prep.

### 9. Keto Paneer Pakora — ~3g net carbs (4 pieces)

Crispy paneer fritters with an almond-coconut batter. Mix almond flour, coconut flour, ajwain, chili powder, chaat masala, turmeric, and a pinch of xanthan gum. Add water to make a thick batter. Dip paneer rectangles, shallow-fry in ghee until golden. Eat immediately — they don't stay crispy long.

### 10. Paneer Tikka Masala — ~5g net carbs

Paneer tikka simmered in spiced tomato-cream gravy. Marinate and grill paneer with peppers and onion (as in recipe #3). Meanwhile, cook tomato puree with butter, ginger-garlic paste, and spices. Add grilled paneer and vegetables, cream, and water. Simmer 5 minutes, finish with kasuri methi.

### 11. Achari Paneer — ~3g net carbs

Tangy, spicy paneer with pickling spices. Heat mustard oil until smoking (removes pungency), then add mustard seeds, fennel, nigella, fenugreek, cumin, hing, and red chilies. When seeds splutter, add ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, and chili powder. Whisk in yogurt carefully (prevents curdling), add paneer, cook 5 minutes.

### 12. Paneer Salad with Tadka — ~4g net carbs

A fresh, crunchy salad with warm spiced ghee. Arrange greens, cucumber, avocado, cherry tomatoes, and pan-fried paneer in a bowl. Heat ghee with mustard seeds, cumin, and red chili until they splutter. Pour the hot tadka over the salad, squeeze lemon, season, and toss.

## Paneer Cooking Tips for Keto

**Don't overcook it.** Paneer needs only 5–7 minutes in a simmering gravy. Overcooking makes it rubbery. Soak store-bought paneer in warm water for 10 minutes before cooking to soften it.

**Pan-fry first.** For kadai and shahi paneer, lightly pan-fry cubes in ghee until golden before adding to gravy. This adds texture and prevents breaking.

**Use full-fat paneer.** Low-fat paneer is rubbery and bland. On keto, you want the fat. Aim for 20%+ fat content.

**Marinate for tikka.** Paneer doesn't absorb marinade as well as chicken. Use thick hung curd (not watery yogurt) and marinate at least 30 minutes.

**Kasuri methi is your secret weapon.** Dried fenugreek leaves add restaurant-style aroma. Crush between your palms before adding, and add at the very end — it burns easily.

**Balance tomato acidity.** Tomato-based gravies can be tangy. Traditional recipes add sugar. On keto, use extra cream, butter, or a pinch of erythritol.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is paneer better than chicken for keto?
They serve different purposes. Chicken is zero-carb and higher in protein per gram. Paneer has more fat and is vegetarian. Both are excellent. Many people eat both — chicken for tandoori dishes, paneer for vegetarian meals and curries. See our [25+ Indian Keto Recipes](/articles/indian-keto-recipes) for both options.

### How much paneer can I eat per day on keto?
200–300g per day is reasonable — that's 36–54g protein and 40–60g fat. One palm-sized serving (100–150g) per meal is a good guideline. If eating paneer at multiple meals, watch total protein — excess can convert to glucose.

### Can I use tofu instead of paneer?
Yes, but tofu has slightly more carbs (~2g net per 100g vs. paneer's ~1g) and less fat. Press it well, pan-fry before adding to gravies, and add extra fat (coconut cream, more oil) to compensate.

### Can I freeze cooked paneer dishes?
Most curries freeze well for up to 2 months — palak paneer, butter masala, and shahi paneer all freeze beautifully. Tikka and pakoras are best fresh; they lose texture when frozen. Thaw in the fridge overnight and reheat gently.

### What's the best paneer brand for keto?
In India: Amul, Mother Dairy, and Gowardhan all make good full-fat paneer (~20g fat per 100g). Outside India: Nanak, Gopi, and Haldiram are common. Always check the label — you want high fat, low carbs, minimal additives.

### Does cooking paneer change its carb count?
No. Paneer's carb content is fixed. What changes the count is the other ingredients — onions, tomatoes, cream, and spices. All recipes here account for those in the net carb totals.
